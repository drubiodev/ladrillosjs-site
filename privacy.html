Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This notice explains what information is collected when you visit the LadrillosJS website, why it is collected, and the choices available to you.

Microsoft Clarity

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use and interact with this website through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay. We use these insights to improve the site's content, navigation, performance, and usability.

Clarity loads only after you select Accept analytics. We grant analytics storage but keep advertising storage denied. Microsoft processes the resulting information as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Information collected

Clarity may collect technical and interaction information such as:

  • Pages visited, navigation paths, and time spent on the site.
  • Clicks, scrolling, and other interactions with page elements.
  • Browser, device, operating system, and approximate location information.
  • Performance, diagnostic, and session information.

This website does not provide user accounts, payment forms, or forms that ask you to submit personal information directly to LadrillosJS.

Cookies and your choices

If you accept analytics, Clarity may use first-party and third-party cookies to connect page views into sessions and measure how the site is used. If you decline, Clarity is not loaded. Your choice is stored in your browser so the site can honor it on later visits.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using . You can also block or delete cookies using your browser settings.

Legal basis and sharing

We rely on your consent to run Clarity. The site shares the usage information described above with Microsoft only after you accept analytics. We do not use Clarity's Identify API or intentionally send names, email addresses, or account identifiers to Clarity.

Microsoft states that Clarity data is stored in Microsoft Azure and may be processed internationally under the safeguards described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Retention

Microsoft currently retains Clarity playback data for 30 days. Click and heatmap data, as well as labeled or favorited sessions, may be retained for up to 9 months. See Microsoft's Clarity data retention documentation for current details.

Your data rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal data, or to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. You may withdraw analytics consent at any time through Cookie settings without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

To exercise rights relating to this site, contact the site maintainer on GitHub. For data controlled by Microsoft, use Microsoft's privacy support and request options linked from its Privacy Statement.
